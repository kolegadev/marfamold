Made by Marfa, the Mould Maker

MarfaMold makes exclusive silicone moulds to order, and a range of ready-to-ship flower moulds besides — for crafters, confectioners and anyone who wants a beautiful shape to work with at home.

Marfa works with high-quality silicone and keeps improving every mould: soft, without cuts, made to reproduce the smallest detail of a flower, and easy to demould.
